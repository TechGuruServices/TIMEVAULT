TimeVault — Privacy Policy

Effective November 19, 2026 · TECHGURU Studio · Missoula, MT

TimeVault is a work hours tracker. This policy explains what data the app handles and what it does not.

Data stored on your device

Your time entries, pay rate, notes, and app settings are stored locally on your own device (browser local storage). They never leave your device unless you turn on cloud sync.

Optional cloud sync

If you choose to enable sync, you supply your own JSONBin.io Master Key and Bin ID in the app's settings, and your entries are stored in that private bin. TECHGURU does not run its own server for TimeVault and cannot see your synced data. You can turn sync off at any time; turning it off stops future syncing but does not delete the bin — delete it from your JSONBin.io account.

What we do not collect

  • No account or sign-in is required to use TimeVault.
  • No analytics, advertising, or third-party tracking.
  • No location data.
  • We do not sell, share, or rent your data to anyone.

Third-party services

PDF export loads the PDF.js library from cdnjs (Cloudflare's public CDN). That request goes to cdnjs only when you export a PDF. Cloud sync, when enabled, uses JSONBin.io as described above. No other third-party services are used.

Children

TimeVault is a work tool intended for adults. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new effective date.
